BUSINESS IMPLICATIONS
Three investigations
follow from the evidence
SFO 2018 / DataSF. My weighted calculations.
09 / 10
Priorities for follow-up, rather than proven improvement
effects:
01
Investigate security pain points
Work with relevant partners to connect complaints
with queue and incident records.
02
Examine dining and retail
Ask about value, availability and service before
deciding which change to fund.
03
Define actual-user measures
Track digital-service access and completion alongside
user-rated experience.
The survey does not quantify revenue uplift or identify which organization
caused each problem.
