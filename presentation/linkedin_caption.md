What can a passenger survey reveal beyond an overall airport rating?

For this customer experience case study, I analysed 2,809 responses from
SFO's 2018 survey in Python.

The findings include:

- 80% of valid respondents rated the airport 4 or 5 out of 5.
- NPS differed by about 43 points between passengers reporting a problem and those reporting none.
- Retail and dining received the lowest top-two service-rating shares.
- Wi-Fi's rating changed substantially when the response base focused on people who used it that day.

The project also explores a less visible part of survey work: interpreting
special codes, defining valid answers, using weights and avoiding double
counting in multi-answer questions.

The accompanying Python project includes an executed notebook, reusable
scripts, a full data audit, sensitivity checks and editable report sources.

Source: San Francisco International Airport / DataSF. These are my weighted
calculations using historical 2018 data. The recommendation gap is an
association, rather than an estimated causal effect of encountering a problem.
